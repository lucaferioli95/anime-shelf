# Changelog

All notable changes to Anime Shelf, newest first. The version number is also shown in the app header.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-03
- First release. A shelf for your anime and manga with three columns: name, score (S, A, B or C) and where to watch or read.
- Type the name and the app suggests matches with their cover (from AniList, with Jikan/MyAnimeList as a backup). Pick one, or just add the name and the best match is used as the cover automatically. Tap a cover to choose a different one.
- Filter by Anime or Manga and by score, search by name, sort by score or name.
- Syncs between your devices when you sign in with Google (same allowed list as the Control Room). Always saved on the device first, works offline for the list.
- Installable, with an install button and the hosting note in the footer.
