# Changelog

All notable changes to Anime Shelf, newest first. The version number is also shown in the app header.

## 2.1.0 - 2026-11-03
- Anime and manga now have different row colours so you can tell them apart at a glance: a soft blue with a blue edge for anime, a soft warm orange with an orange edge for manga (darker versions in dark mode).

## 2.0.0 - 2026-11-03
- Shared shelf: you and a friend now use the same list. Each title records who added it, and every person gives their own score. Your score is the big badge you tap to change; other people's scores show next to it with their name.
- New "To watch / read" list for future reference. Add a title as "To watch / read" (no score needed) and tap "Done" when you have seen it to move it to the shelf.
- "Added by" filter, and the score filter and sorting use your own score.
- Sign-in with Google is now needed to open the app. Only the Google accounts on the shelf's allowed list get in, and they cannot see anything else of yours (the Control Room and Library are separate).
- Anything you had saved on a device before this version is moved to the shared shelf the first time you sign in.
- Only the person who added a title can delete it. Anyone can edit where to watch and the cover.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-03
- First release. A shelf for your anime and manga with three columns: name, score (S, A, B or C) and where to watch or read.
- Type the name and the app suggests matches with their cover (from AniList, with Jikan/MyAnimeList as a backup). Pick one, or just add the name and the best match is used as the cover automatically. Tap a cover to choose a different one.
- Filter by Anime or Manga and by score, search by name, sort by score or name.
- Syncs between your devices when you sign in with Google (same allowed list as the Control Room). Always saved on the device first, works offline for the list.
- Installable, with an install button and the hosting note in the footer.
